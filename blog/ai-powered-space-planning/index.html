AI-Powered Space Planning

May 9, 2024 · Flora Delaney

Watch this webinar featuring Flora Delaney, Delaney Consulting and Dirk Herdes, VP of Retail at Hivery. In this Category Management Association-hosted webinar they explore how AI can help drive hyper-local retail strategies.

This webinar explores actual use cases and explains how AI enables rapid merchandising decisions and execution, all at the store level.

Retailers today want shelves to be locally relevant, effectively merchandised, and operationally efficient. To achieve that, they need to augment their efforts with AI. Delivering have a more rapid and dynamic approach to space and assortment management.

Delaney Consulting conducted a Q1 survey of 56 retailers. Store-specific assortments and planograms were selected as the #1 emerging technology with the most potential for Space Planning. It ranked higher than VR, robotic shelf monitoring, image recognition, and others. For retailers to scale to store-specific planograms, AI will optimize assortments and planograms.

The webinar aired on November 18, 2022.
